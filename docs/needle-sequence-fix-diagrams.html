Needle Sequence Fix
Vial washer · S7-1200 CPU 1214C · needle wash sequence

Why the needles spray on the down stroke, and how to fix it

The machine runs correctly after start, then sprays on the down stroke and stays that way. The diagrams below show the most likely mechanism, the fix in hardware and in the PLC program, and the order to work through it.

CauseThe PLC remembers up/down by toggling on sensor pulses. One missed or doubled pulse inverts it for good.
FixRead real position from separate UP and DOWN sensors, check it every stroke, and stop on a mismatch.
